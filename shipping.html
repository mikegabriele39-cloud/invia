Store Policy

Shipping

Shipping prices and delivery estimates can be updated once your final carrier rates are confirmed.

Processing

Orders are typically prepared for shipment after payment has been successfully confirmed. During launches, drops, or high-volume periods, processing may take longer.

Delivery Estimates

Standard shipping: Estimated 3–7 business days after dispatch.

Express shipping: Estimated 1–3 business days after dispatch.

These are estimates only and are not guaranteed. Carrier delays, weather, holidays, customs, and other factors may affect delivery times.

Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for entering a complete and accurate shipping address at checkout. If you notice an error, contact us as quickly as possible.

Lost or Delayed Packages

If tracking shows an unusual delay or delivery issue, contact us and we’ll help you review the shipment information available from the carrier.

International Orders

International customers may be responsible for duties, taxes, customs fees, or import charges imposed by their destination country.
