Store Policy

Returns & exchanges.

Simple, clear rules for returning your INVIA order.

7-day return window.

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery.

Items must be unworn, unwashed, unused, and in original condition. Original tags must still be attached.

Return shipping

Who pays?

Customers are responsible for return shipping unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect.

Damaged / incorrect

We’ll make it right.

Contact INVIA with your order number and clear photos of the issue as soon as possible.

Refunds

Original payment method.

Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method after the return is received and inspected.

Final sale

Final-sale items.

Items clearly marked final sale cannot be returned or exchanged unless required by applicable law.

Before sending anything back, contact INVIA first so we can give you return instructions.
