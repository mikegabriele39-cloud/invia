Store Policy

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to feel confident in your INVIA order.

7-Day Return Window

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery.

To be eligible, the item must be unworn, unwashed, unused, and in its original condition. The original tags must still be attached.

Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us as soon as possible with your order number and clear photos of the issue. We’ll review it and make it right.

Final Sale

Items marked final sale are not eligible for return or exchange unless required by applicable law.

Refunds

Once your return is received and approved, your refund will be issued to the original payment method. Processing times may vary depending on your payment provider.

Before returning anything, contact INVIA first so we can provide return instructions.
